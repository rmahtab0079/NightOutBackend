Community Guidelines

Be respectful

In Or Out is for planning nights in and out with friends. Treat others the way you want to be treated.

Not allowed

  • Harassment, hate, or threats
  • Sexual content involving minors
  • Spam, scams, or impersonation
  • Illegal activity

Violations can lead to content removal and account ejection, typically within 24 hours of a report.

How to report

On any friend post, tap the menu and choose Report or Block user. Blocking hides their posts from your feed immediately and notifies us.
